Get the sender

The sender runs on the machine that has the file. On that machine:

curl -fsSL https://github.com/cagojeiger/diodecam/releases/latest/download/diodecam-x86_64-unknown-linux-musl.tar.gz | tar xz
./diodecam-x86_64-unknown-linux-musl/diodecam send <FILE>

Other targets: aarch64-unknown-linux-musl, aarch64-apple-darwin, x86_64-apple-darwin. The full guide is on this address on a computer.

Air-gapped server

          
Fetch the archive and its checksum where there is internet, move both through your organisation's approved import path, then verify and unpack on the server.

          
curl -fsSLO https://github.com/cagojeiger/diodecam/releases/latest/download/diodecam-x86_64-unknown-linux-musl.tar.gz
curl -fsSLO https://github.com/cagojeiger/diodecam/releases/latest/download/diodecam-x86_64-unknown-linux-musl.tar.gz.sha256

          
sha256sum -c diodecam-x86_64-unknown-linux-musl.tar.gz.sha256
tar xzf diodecam-x86_64-unknown-linux-musl.tar.gz

          
macOS: downloaded with a browser instead?

          
Browser downloads carry a quarantine mark and macOS refuses an unsigned binary; curl adds no mark. Remove it once:

          
xattr -d com.apple.quarantine ./diodecam-aarch64-apple-darwin/diodecam

          
While sending

          
Keys: + / - fps · space pause · q quit. A larger window is faster (200×55: 1 MiB in about 26 s); resizing mid-transfer is fine. Over 64 MiB: --max-bytes. diodecam -V prints the version.

          
On the phone

          
Safari: Share → Add to Home Screen, and the receiver works offline. It uploads nothing and never opens the file; it is saved only when you tap Share or Download. A closed tab keeps the progress for 7 days and the received file until it is shared or replaced, a day at most. Open the receiver on this computer ›
